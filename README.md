# Picture This

A simple, browser-based bulk photo editing tool.

[**Open Picture This**](https://ayaanahmedstudios.github.io/picture-this/)

## Features
- Bulk photo editing
- Crop, rotate, and resize images
- Light and color adjustments
- Presets and custom looks
- Export in JPEG, PNG, and WebP
- Batch export

## Usage
1. Open Picture This.
2. Upload your photos.
3. Edit your images.
4. Choose your export settings.
5. Download your edited photos.

No installation required. Works directly in your browser.
